Sound source

Where to get one: search freesound.org for "engine rev" and pick a CC0 or CC-BY clip of a single smooth rev from idle to high rpm, or record a car revving in neutral with your phone near the exhaust. 5–15 seconds is ideal. The page measures how the pitch rises, so the sound follows your speed at the right pitch. Your files stay on this phone.

Futuristic

Engines

Setup: mount the phone firmly (any orientation), connect it to the car by Bluetooth, open this page over HTTPS, and tap Start. Pull away normally for a few seconds and the motion sensors lock onto the car's forward direction; until then the sound runs on GPS alone. Use Hold to rev and Demo run while parked to try the engines. For exhaust-behind-you sound, move the car's audio fade toward the rear speakers. Don't touch the phone while driving.
